# Policies

## Refund Policy
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee from the date of purchase. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

## Cancellation Policy
You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time within a billing period, except within the 14-day guarantee window.

## Data Privacy
We do not sell or share customer data with third parties. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Customers can request full data export or deletion at any time by contacting support.

## Support Response Times
- Starter Plan: 48-hour response time (email only)
- Growth Plan: 24-hour response time (email + chat)
- Enterprise Plan: same-day response (dedicated manager)

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
We guarantee 99.5% uptime for all automation workflows. If uptime falls below this threshold in a given month, affected customers receive a 10% credit on their next invoice.
