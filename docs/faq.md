# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does setup take?**
Standard setup takes 5-7 business days. Rush setup (48 hours) is available as a paid add-on.

**Q: Can I switch plans later?**
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Do you offer a free trial?**
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee instead of a free trial, so you can try the service risk-free.

**Q: What CRMs do you integrate with?**
We currently support GoHighLevel, HubSpot, and Salesforce, with more integrations added regularly.

**Q: Is there a setup fee?**
No setup fee on Starter and Growth plans. Enterprise plans may include a custom onboarding fee depending on scope.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my lead limit?**
You'll be notified and can either upgrade your plan or pay a small overage fee ($0.50 per additional lead) for that billing cycle.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings. No long-term contracts required.
